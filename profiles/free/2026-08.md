## Summary

- **Average primary cost (input)**: $0.00/1M — every model is free.
- **Premium models used**: 0.
- **Usage $15 models used**: 0.
- **Free models as primary**: all 20 agents.
- **Expensive models in repetitive phases**: 0.
- **Reasoning ceiling**: only DeepSeek V4 Flash Free has "think modes"; the rest are `none` (MiMo) or `unknown` (Hy3, Nemotron, stealth). This is the core tradeoff of the Free profile.
- **Family separations forced onto weaker models**:
  - P2: verify (MiMo, `none` reasoning) ≠ apply (DeepSeek).
  - P3: judge B (Hy3, `unknown`) ≠ judge A (DeepSeek).
  - P7: refuter (Hy3) ≠ review lenses (DeepSeek ×3, Xiaomi ×1).
- **Soft constraints not satisfiable on the free tier**:
  - review-risk shares family with apply (DeepSeek) — "ideally ≠ apply" unmet.
  - jd-judge-a shares family with the implementer (DeepSeek) — "≠ implementer" unmet.
- **No vision model available**: sdd-design and sdd-onboard run without multimodal input (no free model has vision).
- **Privacy**: every free model may use your data for training during the free period. **This profile is not for sensitive/client code** — see `considerations.md`.
